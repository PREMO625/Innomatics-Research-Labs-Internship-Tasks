============================================================
         JOB DESCRIPTION: DATA SCIENTIST
============================================================

Company   : Everan Solutions
Location  : [City, India / Remote]
Job Type  : Full-Time
Experience: 2+ Years
Posted    : April 2026

------------------------------------------------------------
ABOUT THE ROLE
------------------------------------------------------------
We are looking for a skilled and curious Data Scientist to
join our growing analytics team. You will work on building
predictive models, extracting insights from large datasets,
and communicating findings to business stakeholders.

------------------------------------------------------------
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Develop and deploy machine learning models for business
  use-cases (classification, regression, clustering, etc.)
- Write efficient Python and SQL code for data extraction,
  transformation, and analysis.
- Perform statistical analysis and hypothesis testing to
  validate model assumptions and business insights.
- Build clear, interactive data visualisations and reports
  to communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Collaborate with data engineers, product managers, and
  business analysts across the full data lifecycle.
- Monitor and maintain deployed models; retrain as needed.
- Document methodologies, experiments, and results clearly.

------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
Programming:
  - Python (proficient): NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
  - SQL (intermediate to advanced): complex joins, window
    functions, aggregations; experience with PostgreSQL or
    MySQL preferred.

Machine Learning:
  - Solid understanding of supervised and unsupervised ML
    algorithms: Linear/Logistic Regression, Decision Trees,
    Random Forest, Gradient Boosting (XGBoost/LightGBM),
    K-Means, PCA, etc.
  - Hands-on experience with Scikit-learn for model building,
    cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and evaluation.

Statistics & Mathematics:
  - Strong foundation in probability, statistics, hypothesis
    testing, A/B testing, and regression analysis.

Data Visualisation:
  - Ability to create meaningful charts and dashboards using
    tools such as Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly, or Tableau.
  - Experience translating complex data into clear business
    narratives for diverse audiences.

Data Manipulation:
  - Proficiency with Pandas for data cleaning, reshaping,
    merging, and feature engineering at scale.

Experience:
  - Minimum 2 years of professional experience in a data
    science or closely related analytical role.
  - Demonstrated experience taking an ML project from
    problem framing to production deployment.

Education:
  - Bachelor's degree (or higher) in Computer Science,
    Statistics, Mathematics, Engineering, or a related
    quantitative field.

------------------------------------------------------------
BONUS / NICE-TO-HAVE SKILLS
------------------------------------------------------------
Deep Learning:
  - Familiarity with neural-network frameworks such as
    TensorFlow or PyTorch.
  - Experience with CNNs, RNNs, or Transformer-based models.

NLP (Natural Language Processing):
  - Experience with text preprocessing, sentiment analysis,
    named-entity recognition, or working with LLMs/BERT.
  - Familiarity with HuggingFace Transformers or spaCy is
    a plus.

Cloud Platforms (AWS preferred):
  - Basic hands-on experience with AWS services such as
    S3, EC2, SageMaker, Lambda, or equivalent on GCP/Azure.
  - Understanding of deploying ML models as REST APIs or
    managed endpoints is advantageous.
